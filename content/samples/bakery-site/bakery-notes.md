# Notes for the Marlow Street Bakery website

From Ines Okafor, owner. Typed up after our call on Tuesday.

## Who we are

Marlow Street Bakery is a small neighborhood bakery at 14 Marlow Street, on the corner by the library. Ines Okafor opened it in 2019 with her brother Teo Okafor, who bakes the bread. Everything is baked on site every morning, starting at 4 am. We use a sourdough starter that Teo has kept alive since 2016.

## Opening hours

Tuesday to Friday: 7:00 am to 3:00 pm.
Saturday and Sunday: 8:00 am to 2:00 pm.
Closed on Mondays.

The cardamom buns usually sell out by 11 am on weekends.

## Special orders

Whole cakes and bread for parties need 48 hours' notice. Call us or come by the counter. We do not take orders by email yet.

## What Ines wants from the site

One page. Warm and simple, like the shop: cream and dark brown, nothing flashy. People should see the hours and the menu with prices right away, and how to order a cake. Mention that the bread is baked every morning on site. A small line about Teo's starter would be nice.
