# Launch brief: Fernway, the habit tracker

Written by Maya Lindqvist (product) for the launch team.

## The product

Fernway is a small habit tracker web app: you add habits, tick them off each day and watch your streaks grow. Everything stays in the browser, no account needed.

## Launch date

Public launch on Tuesday 9 February 2027. All launch work happens between 4 January 2027 and 19 February 2027, including one week of follow-up after launch.

## Team

- Maya Lindqvist: product lead, owns the launch and the go/no-go call
- Sam Okoro: developer, owns the app, fixes and the release
- Priya Raman: designer, owns the landing page and the store images
- Leo Martins: marketing, owns the newsletter, the posts and the launch day

## Budget

Total launch budget: $3,000.
- Landing page design and copy: $600
- Paid posts in the first two weeks: $1,500
- Beta tester thank-you gifts: $400
- Launch day event (online): $500

## What must happen

- A closed beta with about 30 testers for two weeks before launch.
- Fix the bugs the beta finds before the release.
- A landing page with a sign-up form, live one week before launch.
- A newsletter to the waiting list on launch day.
- A short launch post and a follow-up post one week later.
- A review of the numbers one week after launch.
