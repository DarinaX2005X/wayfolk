# Hands probe

The three hand actions of the product, and selection, tried on plain bodies in the emulator before any view is built on them (task 01.3.1). The bodies live in the section "Hands probe" of `/gallery` (`/gallery?section=probe`, `src/pages/gallery/probe.ts`); the scenarios read `window.__gw.handProbe` (`log`, `last`, `count`, `open`, `at`). Run them with

```
npm run xr -- tests/xr/probe/1-grab-snap.json tests/xr/probe/2-grab-or-select.json tests/xr/probe/3-pull.json tests/xr/probe/4-reveal.json tests/xr/probe/5-dwell.json tests/xr/probe/6-mouse.json
npm run xr -- tests/xr/probe/7-gaze-glasses.json
```

Bodies, in the seated frame (eyes at the origin, the bench top 0.32 m below): a grey cube 5 cm at −14°, 0.47 m; a target plate 12 × 9 cm at +14°, 0.50 m; a bead 3 cm on a 30 cm segment from 0.95 m toward the seat, 12 cm under the eyes; a slip 6 × 5 cm in a tray at +26°, 0.47 m; a button 44 mm at eye level straight ahead at 0.8 m.

## What works and how

| Action | How it is made in IWSDK | How a scenario does it | What did not work, and what replaced it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Take and carry, snap or return | `OneHandGrabbable` on the handle; a hand pinch next to it grabs (`useHandPinchForGrab`). `Grabbed` on the entity says it is held, `GrabSystem.getHolderHand` which hand. On release `src/interact/grab.ts` pulls it onto a target within 8 cm in 120 ms or back home in 400 ms | mode `hand`: `run` with `target`, `approach`, `pinch: { value: 1, at: "arrival" }`, then `target` of the plate (it sets the held object down on it), then `pinch: 0` | Nothing. The plate must be a target of `xr list-targets` to be carried to by name, so it gets an entity with `RayInteractable` |
| 2. One object: taken near, selected from afar | IWSDK puts `pointerEventsType = { deny: 'ray' }` on the grab node, confirmed: `{"deny":"ray"}` on `probe:cube`; children inherit it. The proxy, a sibling with `RayInteractable` and an invisible material (`material.visible = false`: not drawn, still hit), takes rays, gaze and the mouse | mode `hand-ray`: `run` with `target` and `press`; mode `hand` for the near pinch | Nothing. A near pinch gave a grab and no select; while held the proxy is hidden (`visible = false`), so it cannot answer |
| 3. Pull | `pointerdown` on the bead from a hand ray (`@pmndrs/pointer-events`). The hand of the pointer: `event.pointerId === world.input.xr.multiPointers[hand].getPointer('ray').id`. The wrist: `world.player.raySpaces[hand]` world position. The bead rides toward the seat by the wrist's travel projected on the horizontal axis to the seat; commit at 15 cm; release before: cancel, the bead returns in 400 ms. Release is seen by `pointerup` with pointer capture and by `world.input.xr.getSelectState(hand).active` | `pull` step of `scripts/xr.mjs` (mode `hand-ray`): `{ "pull": { "target": "probe:bead", "by": 0.18, "seconds": 0.7 } }` | Nothing. The step moves the hand along the ray, which also drops a little, so 8 cm of hand travel measured 0.49 of the threshold (7.3 cm on the level axis) |
| 4. Reveal | The held slip's handle is watched: lift (rise plus half of the travel toward the eyes) ≥ 6 cm, or the turn of its normal ≥ 60°, opens; under 3 cm and 35° together, or the release, closes (`src/interact/reveal.ts`) | mode `hand`: lift by `position` relative to the head with `anchor: "grasp"`; turn by `orientation` with `fingers` and `palm` directions | Turning the palm by 65° turned the held slip by only about 57°: the grasp does not turn about the slip's own plane. The scenario turns the palm 80°. Lifting needs no turn at all |
| 5. Dwell (Quest, no eye tracking) | Own pointer (`src/interact/head-pointer.ts`): a ray from `world.player.head` (gaze `eyeSpace` on the glasses, the mouse at the desk), smoothed over 60 ms; the nearest select target with a hit cone of at least 2.2°; arm 250 ms, fill `dwellMs`, select, then 1 s of rest on that target; a 6 mm dot and a filling ring | `head` steps with `recenter: false` turn the headset; `eval` sets `store.settings.dwell` | A head step and an eval each take about half a second through the command line, so "look away before the ring fills" cannot be timed from outside: the scenario checks that looking away resets the ring (`phase` idle, fill 0) and that a select after looking back takes the full 250 + 1200 ms. The setting is remembered on the device, so the scenario sets it off first |
| 6. Mouse at the desk | Canvas events arrive as `pointerType` `screen-mouse`. A click selects; a drag on the proxy carries over a level plane at the object's height (3 cm lifted) and drops like a hand; a bead dragged 120 px down the screen is a pull; the wheel over the slip opens and closes it | mode `mouse`: `mouse` steps with `click` and `drag` (to a target or a point); the wheel through an `eval` that dispatches `pointermove` and `wheel` on the canvas | The canvas pointer type is `screen-mouse`, not `mouse`. The gallery's seat camera keeps at least 60° of height, or the plate would fall outside a small window's canvas |
| 7. Glasses: gaze and pinch | IWSDK's gaze pointer: its id is `world.input.xr.gazePointer.pointer.id`; a pinch of either hand clicks what gaze points at. On the cube that is a select (no grab), on the bead the pull is replaced by a select that commits | device `metaVRGlasses`, mode `gaze`: `run` with `target` and `press` for `hand-right` and `hand-left` | Nothing |

## Notes for the views

- The hand root of the panels and every select target work by `click` of `@pmndrs/pointer-events`; one handler covers ray, fingertip, gaze and mouse. Two selects within 300 ms are one.
- An entity's Object3D must hang directly under another entity's Object3D, or IWSDK moves it to the level root. `ensureEntity` in `src/interact/runtime.ts` makes every ancestor up to the nearest entity an entity.
- uikit takes `lineHeight` as a multiple of the font size, and never `hover: undefined`.
- The managed browser is shared: a scenario can fail with "window.__gw is missing" when another tool reloads the page or edits a watched file. Run it again; a real failure repeats.
