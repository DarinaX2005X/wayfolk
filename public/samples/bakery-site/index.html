Baked here every morning

Everything is baked on site every morning, starting at 4 am.

We use a sourdough starter that Teo has kept alive since 2016.

Ines Okafor opened the bakery in 2019 with her brother Teo Okafor, who bakes the bread.

Special orders: whole cakes and party bread

Whole cakes and bread for parties need 48 hours' notice.

Call us or come by the counter. We do not take orders by email yet.
