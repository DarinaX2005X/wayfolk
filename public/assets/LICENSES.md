# Assets of the app

The app ships no third-party assets of its own: the wordmark, the balloon, the painted sky,
the clouds, the reply bubbles, the postcard and the QR code are drawn in code.

- Atkinson Hyperlegible Next (`public/fonts/`), Braille Institute of America, SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`public/fonts/OFL.txt`), https://www.brailleinstitute.org/freefont/
- The QR encoder (`src/kit/ui/qr.ts`) follows the reference algorithm as laid out by Project Nayuki's QR Code generator library, MIT, https://www.nayuki.io/page/qr-code-generator-library

Assets of the world and life modules have their own `LICENSES.md` in `public/assets/world/` and `public/assets/life/`.
